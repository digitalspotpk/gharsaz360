Privacy Policy

GharSaz 360 — Smart Home, Property & Life Suite

Zero Data Collection · 100% On-Device Storage

Overview

GharSaz 360 ("the App") is an offline-first Progressive Web App. It is designed so that all of your data — budgets, expenses, rent records, ledgers, documents, and every other entry you create — stays stored locally on your own device, inside your browser's local storage. The App does not operate a backend server, and the developer does not receive, store, or have access to any of your data.

What data the App stores, and where

All information you enter (budgets, expense entries, tenant and rent records, Udhar Khata ledgers, construction and labour logs, asset and warranty details, vehicle logs, Zakat and charity records, solar/utility logs, pantry lists, vault documents, event budgets, and savings goals) is saved only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). Nothing is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer.

Document Vault encryption

The Family Emergency & Document Vault module encrypts the sensitive values you store (such as CNIC numbers, IBANs, or policy numbers) using AES-256-GCM encryption, with a key derived from a PIN that only you set and only you know. This encryption happens entirely on your device using your browser's built-in Web Crypto API. The developer cannot recover a lost vault PIN or decrypt your vault data.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to use the optional Google Drive backup feature described in the App's setup guide, your exported backup file is uploaded directly from your device to your own personal Google Drive account using Google's official API and your own Google authentication. The developer's servers are never involved in this process, because the developer does not operate any server the App communicates with.

Data you export

The App lets you export your data as a JSON or CSV file at any time. These files are generated on your device and saved to your device's downloads — you are responsible for storing or sharing these files as you see fit.

Permissions

  • Storage: used only to save your data locally and to cache the App for offline use.
  • The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or SMS.
  • The WhatsApp support button and rent-reminder links open your device's WhatsApp app or browser with a pre-filled message; the App does not read your WhatsApp messages or contacts.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any advertising network, analytics tracker, or third-party data-collection SDK. The only third-party interaction is the optional, user-initiated Google Drive backup described above.

Children's privacy

The App is a household finance and property management tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Data deletion

You can delete all App data at any time from Settings → "Erase All App Data," or by clearing your browser's site data for this App. Because no copy exists on any server, this action permanently and completely removes your data.
